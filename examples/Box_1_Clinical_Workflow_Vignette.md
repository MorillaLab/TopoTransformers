# Box 1: Clinical Workflow Example - Sequential Risk Assessment
Day -30 (Pre-transplant evaluation)
├── Variables: Age=58, COPD etiology, mPAP=32 mmHg, no diabetes
├── No pre-op ECMO, not high emergency
└── **Initial risk score: 0.31** (low-intermediate)

Day 0 (Transplantation)
├── Intra-op: Double LT, cold ischemia=280min, TEA placed
├── Intra-op ECMO used, transfusion >2 units
├── ICU admission: SOFA=8, SAPS2=28
└── **Updated risk score: 0.45** (intermediate)

Day 3 (Early ICU course)
├── On mechanical ventilation, norepinephrine 0.1 μg/kg/min
├── No ECMO, no PGD
├── Acute renal failure develops (creatinine 2.1, oliguria)
├── Antibiotics started for suspected pneumonia
└── **Updated risk score: 0.72** (high) ⚠️

Day 7 (Continued ICU stay)
├── Still ventilated, renal replacement therapy started
├── ECMO initiated for refractory hypoxemia
├── Bronchoscopy: grade 3 PGD
├── No rejection on transbronchial biopsy
└── **Updated risk score: 0.84** (very high) 🔴

Day 14 (Clinical course)
├── ECMO weaned, extubated, off RRT
├── Renal function improving
├── No rejection, no infection
└── **Updated risk score: 0.58** (improving)

Day 30 (ICU discharge)
├── Transferred to ward, ambulating
├── Stable oxygenation, no oxygen requirement
├── Discharged from ICU
└── **Updated risk score: 0.28** (low)

Day 365 (One year post-transplant)
└── **Outcome: SURVIVOR**
